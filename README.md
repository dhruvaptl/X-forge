# ⚒️ X-FORGE

### Career Execution Platform

> **Stop searching for career advice. Start forging your path.**

X-FORGE is a web-based career execution platform designed to help people turn career goals into structured, actionable paths.

Instead of simply telling users what a career is, X-FORGE focuses on what they should **actually do next** — from discovering an unfamiliar career to building skills, completing tasks, tracking progress, and moving toward real-world opportunities.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Many people know what career they are interested in, but don't know:

- Where to start
- What skills they need
- What to do next
- How to stay consistent
- How to track their progress
- What to do while waiting for opportunities
- How to continue growing after entering the field

Traditional career websites mainly provide information.

**X-FORGE focuses on execution.**

---

## 💡 Solution

X-FORGE converts a career goal into a structured journey:

**DISCOVER → EXPLORE → FORGE → EXECUTE → GROW**

Users can explore careers, understand the reality of a profession, create a career path, follow actionable tasks, access resources, and track their progress.

## 🌍 Career Worlds

The current MVP focuses on six less-explored career paths:

| Career | Focus |
|---|---|
| 🍷 Sommelier | Wine, tasting & hospitality |
| 🌊 Oceanographer | Ocean science & research |
| 🧪 Food Researcher | Food science & laboratory research |
| ⚓ Sailor / Deck Officer | Maritime operations & navigation |
| 👑 Pageant Professional | Presentation, competition & performance |
| 🗺️ Cartographer | Mapping, GIS & geographic visualization |

Each career has its own visual identity and roadmap experience.
